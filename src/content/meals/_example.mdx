This file is a copy-paste template for authoring a meal entry. It carries
`visibility: "unlisted"` so it's excluded from listing pages, sitemap, and
RSS, and emits `noindex`.

The body should land on a remembered moment — one specific observation
from the meal — rather than a play-by-play summary. See
[ADR-0012 (#277)](https://github.com/laichunpongben/foodbook/pull/277)
`--tone-anecdotal` for the editorial register.

**Schema cheat-sheet** (defined in `src/content.config.ts`):

- `title` — short editorial title
- `date` — ISO YYYY-MM-DD
- `location` — restaurant slug or the literal `"home"`
- `occasion?` — `"Easter"`, `"weeknight"`, `"tasting menu"`, etc
- `dishes[]` — dish slugs referenced
- `photos[]` — path stems for photos (own kitchen / restaurant shots)
- `heroUrl?` — absolute URL (e.g. Wikimedia) when no local photo
- `tags[]` — from the `dishTags` enum
- `visibility` — `"public"` (default) or `"unlisted"`
- `companionCount?` — integer, no names; per
  [ADR-0005 §visibility](../../../docs/adr/0005-public-by-default-no-private-tier.md)
  names of other people never enter the repo

When authoring a real meal:

1. Copy this file to `src/content/meals/YYYY-MM-DD-short-slug.mdx`.
2. Flip `visibility` to `"public"`.
3. Fill in `title`, `date`, `location` (a real restaurant slug or `"home"`).
4. List the dish slugs you actually ate in `dishes[]`.
5. Write 2–3 sentences of body prose landing on one remembered moment.
6. Add at least one tag from the `dishTags` enum.

Per [issue #317](https://github.com/laichunpongben/foodbook/issues/317), this template
silences the `[WARN] [glob-loader] No files found matching "meals/*.mdx"`
build warning without committing to editorial content.
